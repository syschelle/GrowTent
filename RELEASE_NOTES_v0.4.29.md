# GrowTent v0.4.29

## Release summary

GrowTent v0.4.29 makes a small visual refinement to the compact Operating Settings page. The top-row cards no longer stretch to the height of the tallest card, which removes the awkward empty space inside the Temperature and VPD panels. In addition, a clear vertical gap is restored between the top-row cards and the lower Irrigation Settings section.

## Highlights

- Stops the top-row cards from stretching to a uniform height.
- Lets Grow periods, Temperature, and VPD cards keep their natural content height.
- Restores a visible gap between the top-row cards and the Irrigation Settings section below.
- Keeps all form IDs, save handlers, and logic unchanged.
- Updates firmware and static asset metadata to v0.4.29.

## Compatibility

- No API changes.
- No automation or control-logic changes.
- Layout-only refinement.
